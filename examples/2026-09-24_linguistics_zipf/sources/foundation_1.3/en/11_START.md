# 11. THE START

### The N=1 paradox · The minimum viable graph · What to do on Monday

---

## 0. The problem the whole corpus was avoiding

The protocol is written **for a network.** And a network does not begin with a network.

With one node, almost nothing works: there is no communion, no graph, no enforcement, no one to catch the children, no one to exchange with, nothing to compare the data against. **Ten of the twelve obligations are either degenerate or dead.**

And this is not a philosophical question. It is the question **"what do I do on Monday."** So far the corpus has not answered it.

## 2. A single node is not a cautious beginning. It is SOS

Instinct says: start with one, carefully, debug it, then expand.

**Instinct leads straight into the catastrophe.**

Decompose a single node:

> a closed world + a permanent figure + good intentions + **no external check**

That is an **exact description of SOS Children's Villages** (`10_PRECEDENTS` §1). And we know how it ended: seventy-five years, one hundred and thirty-seven countries, a million children, a founder who was an abuser, a culture of silence, suppressed reports, children expelled for speaking.

> **The small configuration is not the safe one. It is the most dangerous one.**

At N=1 the child has **no one outside.** No other node he has visited and where he is known by name. No node obliged to take him if this one throws him out. No node that will read the open method and break communion. **Every safeguard in this project is other nodes. A single node protects nothing.**

**Hence the founding rule:**

> **A node does not take children until it is in communion with at least two others.**

This is not caution — it is the condition under which the protocol exists at all.

---

## 3. The minimum viable graph

**N=1: nothing.** Enforcement is zero. The child is alone with the institution.

**N=2: worse than nothing.** Formally there is communion. Practically — **mutual hostages**: if A sees abuse at B and breaks communion, A loses **everything** — its only partner, the data pool, the catch, the exchange. **A has an enormous incentive to look away, and B knows it.** Enforcement appears to exist and does not. **A simulated check is more dangerous than none.**

**N=3: the minimum at which a rupture is possible.** A breaks with B — and still has C. Rupture became **affordable**, therefore it became **real.** But fragile: if B and C collude, A is alone.

**N≈7–12: robust.** Cliques become hard, exclusion becomes emergent, bridges appear.

**And a hard additional condition: the nodes must be DIFFERENT.**

Three identical nodes founded by the same people, with one faith and one source of money, are **one node in three buildings.** No variation, no independent check, no selection. A shell game played against oneself.

**Required:** different faiths, different founders, different jurisdictions, different money. **The difference between nodes *is* the check.**

---

## 4. The real start is not a node. It is software

**The free tier (`08_DATA` §5) is not monetization. It is the bootstrap.**

It **has no N=1 problem, because it has no nodes.** An ordinary family, an ordinary child, an AI companion and the erasure game. It works at N=0.

What it gives **immediately**:

- **the child's AI and the erasure game** — working for one family, one child, today;
- **data** — and this is at once the **control population**, without which nothing else can be interpreted;
- **revenue** — the recommender (`07_ECONOMY` §5);
- **a community** from which node founders will later grow: families who want more; grandfathers who want grandchildren; corporations who need people.

**And this can begin on Monday.**

---

## 5. The sequence

1. **The protocol is published.** Open, forkable, and **the author owns nothing.**
2. **The child's AI + the erasure game.** Open source, runs locally, free (`07_ECONOMY` §4 — the Linux model).
3. **The recommender — in fast domains.** Consumables: repurchase gives a signal within weeks. **Do not touch slow domains (school, house, doctor, pension) until there is a trace** — there you are blind and corruptible (`02_COMMUNION` §6-bis).
4. **Data accumulates.** The dataset has begun. The control population grows.
5. **Nodes grow out of the users.** Not "we will build a network" — but "the people who need it will build it themselves."
6. **The first three nodes are different, independent, and in communion with each other BEFORE the first child is taken.**

---

## 6. Anti-Gmeiner: the protocol's author does not found a node

The most dangerous thing the author can do is **become a founder.**

SOS: one charismatic founder, a halo of sanctity, forty years of silence after his death — **and he turned out to be an abuser** (`10_PRECEDENTS` §1). A sanctified founder **is** the mechanism of the catastrophe.

> **The author publishes the protocol and steps away.**

He is not a member of the network. He holds no node. He has no voice in communion. He does not "guide." **As the author of an RFC does not run the internet.**

This follows directly from M7 and is a direct refusal of the Second Foundation (`README`). If the author wants a node, let him found one **under his own name, as one among many, with not a single privilege.** But better not.

**A testable sign of failure:** a **founder figure** appears in the texts, at conferences, in the press. The moment the network has a face, it has a center — and everything then proceeds on the SOS timetable.

## 7. Who writes version 1.4

**The RFC model: rough consensus and running code.**

Anyone may propose a change. Nodes adopt it or they do not. **The version most nodes run *is* the standard.**

No committee. No vote. No editor-in-chief. **Forking is legitimate and expected** (`01_PROTOCOL` §7).

| What | When |

---

## 9. The first cohort — and this must be said aloud

**The first children grow up in a system that knows nothing yet.**

We **cannot** check whether what we are doing works: the data does not exist **by construction**; it will exist in twenty years. The first cohort **pays for our ignorance.**

**This is not solvable.** It is the price of ever knowing anything — and children of every generation have paid it; it simply was not written into the charter before.

**What we are obliged to do:**

- **observe, do not experiment** (`08_DATA` §6): randomized interventions only with explicit consent, and minimally;
- **maximal openness**: the method in plain view, outside eyes inside, nothing hidden;
- **minimal ambition**: not "raise a new human being" but **do not break an ordinary one**;
- **three nodes from day one** — because the only protection of the first cohort is other nodes.

**The formula of this file.** We should have started yesterday — but **not with a node.** A lone node with a permanent figure, good intentions and not one witness outside is not a cautious first step; it is **a ready-made SOS**, and we know how that ends after seventy-five years. What is built first is **the software**, because it works at zero nodes and because the child needs it in its own right. And a node appears only when there are two **foreign** nodes beside it, ready to look at you — because **every safeguard in this project is other people.**
