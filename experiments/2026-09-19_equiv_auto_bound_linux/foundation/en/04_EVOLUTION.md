# 04. EVOLUTION

### The engine of the project

---

## 0. The thesis

The project does not design the correct human being. The project creates **an environment in which the correct will find itself** — by selection, not by design.

This is the one file about *why* everything is built as a protocol rather than a charter. The other files describe the mechanism; this one explains why the mechanism is the way it is.

---

## 1. Where the working thing comes from at all: hermeticism → science

The argument "we do not know the correct answer, therefore we have no right to dictate" is best proven by the history of science itself.

Modern science grew not from a correct method handed down from above, but from **hermeticism** — alchemy, astrology, kabbalah, the search for the philosopher's stone and the world soul. Newton wrote more on alchemy and the interpretation of Revelation than on physics. Kepler sought the music of the celestial spheres. Boyle believed in the transmutation of metals. Chemistry grew from alchemy; astronomy from astrology; medicine from folk healing.

The conclusion is hard: **what is born already correct and complete is sterile and leaves no offspring. What is born dirty but able to change gives rise to worlds.**

You could not, in 1600, select the "correct" chemistry and ban alchemy: correct chemistry did not yet exist; it could only grow out of the incorrect, through hundreds of iterations, each a little less wrong. An inquisitor who declared alchemy heresy would have killed chemistry in the womb — not because he defended a falsehood, but because he **weeded out the variants before selection had sorted them.**

**My earlier ban on surrogacy (version 1.2) was exactly this.** An attempt to select the correct form of reproduction before evolution had found it. An inquisition declaring heresy the very thing from which, a hundred iterations on, the working form might have grown. Version 1.3 removed that ban — not out of liberalism, but out of understanding the mechanism: **selecting content at the start sterilizes evolution before it begins.**

Democracy lives because it can change without blood. The internet lives because it mutates without permission. Science lives because each of its results is refutable. None of the three is "correctly built"; all three are **able to evolve.** That is the one thing distinguishing the living from the dead.

## 2. Evolution as the "quantum computer of reality"

The metaphor is exact, not poetic, and worth unfolding.

A quantum computer executes all variants in parallel and extracts the needed one by measurement. Evolution does the same: **each node is a separate executing variant**; they all run at once; the environment "measures" them — the unfit collapse, the fit remains and is copied (M10).

The key property: evolution **does not search** for the answer and **does not know** it in advance — it *finds it by executing*, trying everything at once. There is no gap here between question and answer: **selection is the answer.** That is exactly why it "always produces the correct variant" — not because it is wise, but because "correct" is **defined as the survivor.** A tautology? No — therein lies the whole strength: a system that need not *know* the correct answer to *obtain* it is invulnerable to ignorance. And ignorance of the correct way to raise children is the project's founding condition (`16_OBJECTIONS`: we have no psychohistory).

Hence the architect's role: he **does not compute the answer for the computer.** He sets the rules of selection and steps aside. Every time he writes in a substantive rule, he pulls a variant out of superposition by hand — and thereby steals part of the search from the computer. That is why the rules are minimal.

---

## 3. The central caveat: the fit ≠ the correct. Cancer is fit too

Here is the most important thing in the whole file, and what protects the project from degenerating into the slogan "let the strongest win."

**Evolution does not produce the correct answer. It produces the fit one. What counts as fit is set by the environment.**

A tumor is **fit**: it grows faster than its neighbours, evades the defenses, wins inside the organism — and dies with it. It won selection in an environment where "fitness" = "divide faster." The difference between evolution toward life and evolution toward cancer lies **not in the fact of selection, but in the environment that was set.**

Hence the corollary that overturns the project's naive Darwinism: **the environment is the protocol.** The few rules wired into `01_PROTOCOL` are not a limit on evolution but its **landscape**, setting what counts as survival here.

- Without **M8** (free exit), the "fit" would be the node that better *retains* people: a cult. Free exit makes fit the one people do not leave out of love, not out of inability to leave.
- Without **M9** (the long trace), the fit would be the node that *sells best today*: marketing instead of results. The long trace makes fit the one whose grown children live well.
- Without **M7** (anti-monopoly), the fit would be the one who *ate the others* first: cancer. Anti-monopoly forbids precisely the cancerous strategy.
- Without **M1** (attachment), the fit could be the node made cheapest by dropping the permanent figure: a conveyor. The attachment axiom places that strategy outside the viable.

**Therefore the protocol must be minimal — but must exist.** Zero rules is not freedom, it is cancer: an environment where the most predatory wins. Many rules is the death of evolution: an environment where you cannot mutate. The art is in the minimal set that shapes a landscape "toward life" without dictating the route.

We do not touch content. We **create the selective pressure** and leave. This is not "non-intervention" — it is the only meaningful intervention: not in the players, but in the rules of the game.

## 4. What is not surrendered to evolution: one axiom and one principle

From §3 it follows that almost everything can be handed to selection — but not everything.

**The M7 principle (anti-monopoly) is not surrendered.** Because evolution has no built-in defense against a monopolist — on the contrary, concentration is its own attractor (§3, cancer). Anti-monopoly cannot be a *result* of selection, because selection would eat it first. It must lie *before* selection and be defended *from* it — as distribution is wired into the internet from above, not "won a competition." This is the single point where the architect stands **above** the process.

**The M1 axiom (attachment) is not surrendered.** Not because selection would not find the answer, but because it would find it **too dearly**: the price would be paid by specific broken children, and they did not choose to be in the experiment (`05_ATTACHMENT`). Here irreversibility outweighs the speed of selection. The single substantive exception is paid for by the single irreversibility.

Everything else — surrogacy, methods, ideologies, ways of life, aesthetics, regimes, the very interpretation of the prohibitions — **is surrendered to evolution entirely.** One axiom, one inviolable principle, and beyond that the computer of reality computes for itself.

---

## 5. Why "you cannot prohibit what you cannot control"

A direct consequence of all the above, and at the same time the answer to "but what about bad nodes."

A prohibition that cannot be enforced is **worse than no prohibition**: it does not stop the prohibited thing but creates the lie that it has been stopped, and drives it into invisibility. How will you monitor what a node in Cuba does? You won't. So a substantive prohibition in the network's charter is self-deception: the network has and can have no body to enforce it (`01_PROTOCOL` §0).

Therefore the project **does not prohibit** — it sets **conditions of connectivity** and an **environment of selection.** A bad node is not "banned" — it is cut off from data, insurance and money (`02_COMMUNION`), withers and stays marginal, and its children are caught (M5). This is not impotence — it is the *only* form of influence that creates no center. And it works not because we are kind, but because that is how everything living works: the internet did not "ban" spam — it made the spammer unprofitable. Evolution does not "ban" a bad mutation — it does not let it reproduce.

**The state, meanwhile, has not gone anywhere.** A node is a subject of the criminal law of its jurisdiction. The network does not replace the police and does not shield anyone from it. The network is not the police. It is an environment of selection built on top of ordinary law, not in place of it.

---

## 6. Summary

- The working thing is not designed — it grows by selection out of the non-working (hermeticism → science).
- Evolution is a parallel search over all variants with selection-as-measurement; it need not know the answer to find it.
- But it produces the **fit, not the correct**, and fitness is set by the environment. Cancer is fit too.
- **The environment is the protocol.** Minimal rules are not a brake on evolution but its landscape, distinguishing "life" from "cancer."
- Exactly two things are not surrendered to evolution: **anti-monopoly** (else selection slides to monopoly) and **attachment** (else the price is paid by one who did not choose). Everything else is surrendered.
- You cannot prohibit the uncontrollable; you can set the environment. The bad is not punished — it turns out to be non-viable.

**We are not building the correct upbringing. We are building evolution in the segment of human culture where it has never existed. The correct will be found by it — twenty years on, in graduates' outcomes, without us.**
