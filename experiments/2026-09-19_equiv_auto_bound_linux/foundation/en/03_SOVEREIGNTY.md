# 03. NODE SOVEREIGNTY

### Whose the land, his the faith

---

## 0. The principle

*Cuius regio, eius religio* — "whose the realm, his the religion." The formula that ended a religious war at Augsburg in 1555: not by making everyone believe alike, but by granting each prince the right to set the faith on his own land. Not agreement on substance — **agreement on boundaries.**

Here it means literally this:

**A node brings the land — money, premises, jurisdiction, people.
A node holds the faith — method, way of life, ideology, regime, aesthetics, selection.
The network holds no opinion on either.**

**And anyone may found a node — no permission is required, because there is no one to grant it.** A corporation, a family, a city, a state, a university, a monastery, a trade union, a co-operative — or a two-hundred-person factory that simply decided to. **Entry is not granted; entry is recognized** by other nodes through communion (`01_PROTOCOL` §3). Hence "let a thousand flowers bloom" — not a slogan but a **working condition of selection**: the first generation of nodes is not selected but appointed, so the only hedge against a blind start is **maximal variance.**

Everything not among the twelve obligations of the protocol (`01_PROTOCOL`) is sovereign. Not "tolerated," not "permitted with reservations," but **outside the network's competence** — as the content of a letter is outside the competence of a mail server.

---

## 1. Why this is not softness but load-bearing structure

The temptation is to write in "just a little" more common rules: a recommended pedagogy, a quality standard, a charter of values. Each point looks reasonable alone. But **each requires a body to interpret and apply it** — and a body is a center, a center is a monopoly, and monopoly kills evolution (`04_EVOLUTION`).

The logic is hard and one-directional: **any common substantive rule creates a center. Therefore the number of substantive rules must be zero** — except the single one paid for by irreversibility (M1, attachment, `05_ATTACHMENT`).

Node sovereignty is not a concession to diversity for its own sake. It is the **refusal to build the very thing the project swore not to build.** A network with no opinion on content cannot be captured through content — because there is nothing to capture.

---

## 2. What exactly the node decides (and no one but the node)

**How to bear children.** Naturally, IVF, surrogacy, gamete donation, future technologies. Surrogacy is legitimate everywhere and without reservation (`07_ECONOMY`). No "approved method" exists.

**Who bears, and for whom.** Family composition: couples of any composition, singles, plural configurations, donors, commissioning parents. The node defines its own model.

**How to raise.** Pedagogy, regime, discipline, curriculum, language, religion, ideology, aesthetics, relationship to technology, to the body, to sex, to work. The whole corpus of upbringing is sovereign.

**In what form to live.** Separate campuses, dormitories, company towns, city blocks, scattered flats, a village, a monastery. **M1 constrains the night, not the building:** the child sleeps with his apex. A campus with a shared kitchen, a shared school and shared daytime care, where at night the child is with his apex, is **a kibbutz minus the children's house** — a kibbutz with its single error corrected (`05_ATTACHMENT` §3). A company town with family housing is **a village with a payroll**; this was once known how to do.

**What to believe.** A node has a worldview, and it may be anything (see §3).

**Whom to admit.** Selection of staff, participants, families, children — the node's criteria. A node may be as selective as it likes at the entrance; the one thing it does not control is freedom of exit (M8).

**How to earn.** Any model legal in its jurisdiction (`07_ECONOMY`).

**How to interpret the three prohibitions.** M2 (violence/drugs/propaganda) is deliberately undefined — interpretation belongs to the node, disputes end in rupture of communion, not a court (`02_COMMUNION` §6).

**What data** to collect beyond the mandatory exchange minimum (`08_DATA`).

## 3. Different faiths — all within their right

The network not only permits incompatible worldviews — it **relies on them**: these are the variants evolution explores. Below are not recommendations or models but illustrations of range. Each such node is legitimate exactly insofar as it keeps the twelve obligations — and not one jot less legitimate for a "wrong" faith.

- **A religious node.** Faith, rite, community, sacred texts at the base of upbringing. A dense norm. — Legitimate. The boundaries are the same: the child may leave at majority with zero debt (M11), exit is free (M8), attachment is kept (M1).
- **A secular rationalist node.** Science, critical thinking, no metaphysics. — Legitimate on the same terms.
- **A techno-node.** Early integration with technology, AI tutors, quantitative developmental tracking, optimization for the future. — Legitimate; the red line is that the child's data belongs to the child, no total archive (`08_DATA`, `15_MIRROR`).
- **A national / cultural node.** Preservation of a language, tradition, identity being eroded by globalization. — Legitimate, **as long as it defines itself by what it preserves, not against whom** (a condition from `16_OBJECTIONS` §in-group, not a separate prohibition).
- **Families of any composition.** Single parents, same-sex couples, plural care configurations. — Legitimate on the same terms as all others. The network holds no opinion on family composition.
- **A commune / kibbutz-like node.** Maximal collective care. — Legitimate under one condition: the collective does not abolish the permanent attachment figure and does not take the child at night (M1 — the very line on which the historical kibbutzim broke, `05_ATTACHMENT`).

**The common denominator is exactly one:** the twelve obligations. Everything else is faith, and there are as many faiths as nodes. The network does not rank faiths by correctness — evolution ranks them, twenty years on, by graduates' outcomes (M9).

---

## 4. Autocephaly: sovereignty is not isolation

The model is the autocephalous Churches (`02_COMMUNION`): each fully self-governing within, yet **in communion** with the others. Node sovereignty is of the same kind: full power over its content **plus** ties outward.

Sovereignty does **not** mean:
- closedness (a node closed on exit violates M8 and falls out);
- refusal to exchange data (violates M4);
- refusal to catch others' children (violates M5);
- secrecy of method (violates M10).

That is, the node is sovereign in **content** but bound by the **protocol of connectivity**. This is no contradiction: a mail server fully controls its own mailboxes but must speak SMTP or it is connected to no one. Faith is sovereign. The protocol is binding. One does not hinder the other; together they yield a network, not an archipelago of sects.

---

## 5. The boundary of sovereignty is exactly the twelve obligations

So there is no illusion about where "do as you wish" ends:

| The node decides (faith) | The node must (protocol) |
|---|---|
| How to bear, who bears | M1: permanent attachment figure |
| How to raise, what to teach | M2: no violence/drugs/propaganda (interprets itself) |
| What to believe, which ideology | M3: public communion list |
| Which discipline, regime, aesthetics | M4: data exchange |
| Whom to admit to staff and families | M5: catch others' children |
| How to earn | M6: fund itself |
| How to interpret the M2 prohibitions | M7: build no monopoly (incl. genome) |
| Which language, culture, religion | M8: free exit |
| How much data to collect beyond the minimum | M9: publish the long trace |
| Which technologies, which relation to the body | M10: open the method |
| Aesthetics, way of life, rhythm | M11: release the grown without debt |
| Everything not in the right column | M12: name receivers for the children |

**The left column is infinite. The right is closed and short.** That is the whole construction: maximum freedom of content under a minimum of connectivity rules.

---

## 6. Why a diversity of faiths removes the "aliens"

A common objection (fully treated in `16_OBJECTIONS`): children raised "differently" will become alien to the rest of society — a new sect, new "not like everyone."

The answer is right here, in sovereignty: **aliens are produced by monoculture — a single center stamping out a single template.** The network has no center and no template. It has hundreds of nodes with hundreds of incompatible faiths. A child from a religious node and a child from a techno-node are no more like each other than each is like an "ordinary" child outside.

A diversity of faiths *is* the defense against alienness: **you cannot be alien among everyone if "everyone" is a motley network of the different, not one stamp.** Not "the people of Foundation" as a type, but simply children raised in different traditions — as all of humanity has been until now. The only difference is that these traditions are new, compete openly, and exchange data.

---

**The formula of this file.** The network is not "how children ought to be raised." The network is the guarantee that **no one decides how they ought to be** except the node itself and, twenty years on, evolution. Whose the land, his the faith. The faiths differ. The land is each one's own. The connection is common. There is no center.
